Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PPSC Point ("we", "our", "the website") collects when you visit https://www.ppscpoint.site, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

This is a static educational website. We do not require registration and we do not collect names, passwords, phone numbers or payment information. The only personal information we receive is what you voluntarily send us by email (for example, your email address and message) when you contact us.

Like almost all websites, our hosting provider automatically records standard log data — your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the date/time of your visit. These logs are used only for security, troubleshooting and aggregate statistics.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them in two ways:

  • Essential/preference cookies — for example, remembering your light/dark display choice on this website.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google and its partners, as explained in Section 3.

You can disable cookies at any time in your browser settings. The website will still work, although your display preference may not be remembered and ads may be less relevant.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements, which keeps this website free for students.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick/DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • For more detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand which subjects and topics are most used, so we can improve the content. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

5. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website and its MCQ content;
  • To respond to your emails and correction reports;
  • To display advertising that supports the free service;
  • To protect the website against abuse and technical problems.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to anyone. Information may be shared only with the service providers who help run this website (hosting and advertising — for example Google, as described above) or when required by law.

7. External Links

Our pages may link to external websites (for example, official commission websites). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

This website provides general educational material and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Security and Retention

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your matter. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we hold.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version, and continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact About Privacy

For any privacy question, write to contact@ppscpoint.site.
