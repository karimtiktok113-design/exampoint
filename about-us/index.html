About Us

Welcome to PPSC Point

PPSC Point is a free online test-preparation platform for students and job seekers across Pakistan. We provide 58,639 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) with verified answers for the FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, KPPSC, BPSC, AJKPSC, NTS, CSS, PMS and other competitive examinations conducted in Pakistan.

What We Offer

  • Subject-wise MCQs in English, Pak Study, Islamic Studies, Mathematics, General Knowledge, Everyday Science, Current Affairs, Urdu, Computer.
  • Topic-wise practice — every subject is divided into focused topics so you can prepare one area at a time.
  • A dedicated page for every question, with its correct answer and related MCQs.
  • Instant answer checking — click any option on a practice page to reveal the correct answer.
  • Free online tests with instant scoring, and full-text search across all 58,639 MCQs.

Our Content

Our MCQs are compiled from past papers of Pakistani testing agencies and standard preparation books. Every question is published with its correct answer, and answers are reviewed for accuracy. If you ever find a mistake, please tell us through our Contact page — corrections from readers help thousands of other students.

Our Mission

Quality test preparation in Pakistan is often locked behind expensive books and academies. Our mission is simple: the best preparation material should be free, organised and available to everyone, on any device, at any time.
